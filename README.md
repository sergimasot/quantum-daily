# The Quantum Daily

A weekday newspaper of the quant-ph arXiv listing: **https://sergimasot.github.io/quantum-daily/**
([past editions](https://sergimasot.github.io/quantum-daily/archive.html))

Every morning it forecasts which of the new papers the community will rate most highly, checks the previous day's forecast against the actual ratings, and adds the significant quantum computing news and upcoming conferences and deadlines.

It is compiled automatically by Claude, an AI model, from arXiv, SciRate and news sources. Summaries can contain mistakes, so read the paper before citing it. Not affiliated with arXiv or SciRate.

## When it comes out

Each weekday by about 08:30 Swiss time. arXiv announces new listings Sunday to Thursday at 20:00 US Eastern (about 02:00 in Switzerland), so each edition covers the listing announced overnight. Monday's covers Sunday night's listing, and weekend news. On Fridays the paper is updated around 10:30 with a review of the week.

## What the ranking means

[SciRate](https://scirate.com/arxiv/quant-ph) lets researchers "scite" papers they think are worth reading. After a day or two, the scite count is a good measure of how much attention a paper gets.

At 8:00 the new papers have hardly any scites, so **Today's Picks** are ranked by a *forecast* of their final count. The forecast weighs the significance and novelty of the result, the track record of the authors and groups, how active the topic is, and lessons from comparing earlier forecasts with what happened. #1 is the paper we expect to be scited most. It is a forecast of attention, not a verdict on quality. Each pick says why it is ranked where it is.

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ●●● High | We expect it in the listing's top five. |
| ●●○ Medium | Likely in the top ten. |
| ●○○ Low–Med / Low | Plausible but uncertain: often a specialised or slow-burning paper we think deserves a look. |

| Scite badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `12 EARLY` | Count when the paper was written, a few hours after announcement. Unreliable. |
| `47 FINAL` | Count after about a day and a half, close to settled. |
| `—` | No count yet. |

## Sections

- **Front page**: the day's most important item, usually the top pick, plus the deadline watch.
- **Today's Picks**: the forecast ranking, with a summary of each paper and why it is ranked there.
- **Yesterday, Settled**: the previous listing's actual top papers. *We said* is the rank we had forecast; the scorecard counts how many of our top five made the real top five; *Worth a second look* summarises high scorers we missed. Without counts, the section lists the previous picks instead.
- **Last Week in Quantum** (Fridays): the week's top papers and the forecast record for each day.
- **Industry & Policy**: only significant news. Major news moves to the front page; minor items become one-line briefs.
- **Deadline watch / Conferences**: days left to deadlines in the next eight weeks (red when two weeks or less), and upcoming meetings. Checked weekly.
